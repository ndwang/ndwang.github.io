I’m a physics PhD student at Cornell University, with research in simulation, optimization, and machine learning for particle accelerators. I work across the AI stack, from CUDA and GPU optimization to model training, inference, and agent systems.

My recent work includes models of physical dynamics and tools for automated research and agent evaluation. During my internship at Shizuku AI, I built a real-time multimodal agent harness and optimized text-to-speech inference, reducing time to first audio from 600 ms to 180 ms.

I expect to graduate in 2027 and am looking for full-time AI research and engineering roles. I’m particularly interested in systems that can run experiments, evaluate results, and improve through iteration.
